Verification notes
---------------------
- Corpora that are DUA/LDC-gated (aquaint, car v1.5/v2.0, gov, gov2,
  clinicaltrials 2017-2021) can't be `--verify`'d without the real data;
  only their freely-downloadable queries/qrels were verified against
  upstream trec.nist.gov/etc. counts in ir_datasets/etc/metadata.json.
- highwire/medline aren't DUA-gated, but weren't `--verify`'d against live
  downloads either (multi-hundred-GB corpora); their count_hints come from
  the exact counts already asserted in test/integration/highwire.py and
  test/integration/medline.py (not re-derived by hand).
- None of the other ~25 modules in the Done section below (argsme, c4,
  clueweb09/12, csl, dpr_w100, istella22, kilt, natural_questions, cord19,
  nyt, pmc, sara, codesearchnet, touche/touche_image, trec_fair, trec_tot,
  wapo, tripclick, tweets2013_ia, wikiclir, trec_cast, aol_ia) are attested
  in manifest.json either -- this was undocumented, not actually verified.
  As a weak proxy, most of their count_hint literals do overlap the
  corresponding v1 test/integration/*.py counts, except:
    - clueweb09.py/clueweb12.py: no numeric overlap found at all.
    - wikiclir.py: count_hints are computed from variables, not literals,
      so overlap couldn't even be checked this way.
    - sara.py: 2 of 3 counts (docs/qrels) actually MISMATCH the v1
      integration test -- likely because v2 now points at a different
      upstream (Zenodo) than the old test fixture. Worth checking directly,
      this could be a real bug rather than just missing documentation.
  Revisit all of these (proper `--verify` runs) later.
- After any node rename/split, refreeze with:
    python3 -m ir_datasets.v2.freeze --only irds:<name1>,irds:<name2>,...
  then confirm no nodes were unexpectedly removed:
    python3 -c "import json,subprocess; new=json.load(open('ir_datasets/v2/manifest.json')); old=json.loads(subprocess.check_output(['git','show','HEAD:ir_datasets/v2/manifest.json'])); print(set(old['nodes'])-set(new['nodes']))"
